A LETTER FROM MY HEART

My Dearest Love,

I don't know where to begin, because there are so many things I want to tell you. But one thing I know for sure is that meeting you has become one of the most beautiful parts of my life.

Thank you for being someone who makes ordinary days feel special. Your smile, your laughter, and even the little things you do mean more to me than you could ever imagine.

I know that love isn't always perfect. There will be difficult days, misunderstandings, and moments when words are not enough. But I want you to know that my feelings are genuine, and I will always value what we have.

If I could choose again, I would still choose to know you, to care for you, and to create beautiful memories with you.

You will always have a special place in my heart. No matter how simple these words may seem, every sentence comes from a sincere heart.

With all my love,

Someone who loves you ♡
